# Credits

The Matter Library's content is CC0: no one is *required* to credit anyone. We credit people and sources anyway, because a community library runs on it. **If you use the library, we ask** that you credit it too, for example:

> Materials from the **Matter Library** (https://github.com/Imrsv-tools/Matter-Library), CC0.
