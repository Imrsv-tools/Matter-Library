# Research — The platform's notice on skin making: it moves to the project that makes the body, and is parked (2026-11-17)

**Mnemonic:** `SMK` — reports `R-SMK-n` (for the ledger), asks `Q-SMK-n` (for the library).
**Mode:** an asks doc, written from the platform's side. It commits the library to nothing.
**Asked by:** the platform's lead, 2026-11-17, on setting up a separate project for making human bodies: *"Infrom the neighbors"*.
**Public repo:** needs and uses only. No consumer paths, code or tracker numbers.

**Read against:** this repo at `0a1a974` — its Roadmap (*Phase28 — AI Hands and Feet*, paused; *Skin Layers*; *Eyes in the Shader*) and its completed skin phases 21 to 25 by name only. The skin specs themselves were not re-read for this notice.

**Passes:** the reports and asks below are the platform's (Pass 1, 2026-11-17). The library's reading, the open questions and `## Resolved` belong to the doc.

## Reports — for the ledger

**R-SMK-1 — The platform's lead has set up a separate project that makes human bodies.**
- It makes one human mesh for every age, the shapes that make it a particular person, and the eyes, teeth and other separate pieces inside it.
- It is run by the platform's own team first. Nothing has been made there yet.

**R-SMK-2 — The body's mesh is going to change, and with it the layout every skin is drawn for.**
- The mesh the lead has ruled is in use for now is a different, denser one than the body the library's skins are made for. Its map layout is two tiles, head and body, not one square.
- Which mesh is final is not decided. Until it is, the layout a new skin would be drawn for is not known.

**R-SMK-3 — Making skins moves to that project, and is parked.**
- Asked whether the making of skins belongs with the making of the body, the lead said yes, and: *"park the skin for now - we cannot make progress on skins until we are sure we have a fully complete, high fidelity mesh (or set of meshes or whatever) that we will use to make skins - Skins will be AI generted and need to be trained and we will need to create the tooling for that just like we need make the toolking for meshes.. I do not want to confuse these two or try to do them concurrent"*.
- The reason given to him: a skin is drawn for one mesh's layout and has to agree with the body it is on, its age and its build. A skin method built before the mesh is settled is built twice.

**R-SMK-4 — The library stays the shelf.**
- Finished skins are still to be published here as materials with their pictures, as they are today.
- The skins the platform uses now stay in use: `Skin_Tone34` and the six `Skin_Fitzpatrick I–VI`.

## Asks — for the library

**Q-SMK-1 — Hold new skin-making method work here until the lead re-opens skins.**
- That is: drawing a skin's maps with an image model, training one, and new capture or mapping rigs for skins. *Phase28 — AI Hands and Feet* is already paused; from the platform's side it stays so.
- Whether to mark anything in this Roadmap, and how, is the library's call.

**Q-SMK-2 — Keep what exists findable.**
- When skins re-open, the work will start from what was learned here: the skin map specification, the pigment-based skin generator, the makeup inputs, the completed skin phases and their research. Nothing is asked to be moved or copied now.

**Q-SMK-3 — A question for then, not now: a second layout.**
- A skin for the new mesh will have a different layout from today's. Whether the library's skin materials then carry one layout, two, or a new one is open, and is not asked to be answered yet.

**Not asked:** any change to materials, makeup pieces, eyes, hair colour or the masters. The platform's makeup and skin detail pieces keep working on today's skins.
