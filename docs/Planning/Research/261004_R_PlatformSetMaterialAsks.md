## The short version

- **Four draft materials are wanted:** a brushed aluminium, a cool-white light strip, a polished concrete, and a fine powder coat. Two more would be nice: a terrazzo and a frosted glass.
- **All six are already rows in the Library Coverage draft list,** with a name, a master and a lane. The ask is to bring those rows forward, not to invent anything.
- **One tooling need:** the Creator Asset Profile gate cannot pass an asset that holds a single mesh.
- **The Set already exists as a sketch** in the released materials, so the library can see exactly where each new one goes and at what size.

## Pass 1 — What the Set is, and what it needs

*Written from the platform's side, 2026-10-04.*

### The Set, as it touches the library

- **SM-F1 — It is a round pavilion about 56 m across,** generated by a script: a ribbed dome 38 m wide and 12 m high with an 8 m ring open to the sky, open all round below 4 m, a ring of terrace outside it, and a closed dark drum on one side. It is seen at eye height in a headset, and from above at dollhouse scale.
- **SM-F2 — It is built from repeated pieces.** About 250 placements of about 36 unique meshes, most of them instances. Each piece is one mesh with one Matter material, in the Creator Asset Profile's lightweight form: bare `@Name.mtlx@`, identity on `assetInfo:identifier`, `subdivisionScheme = "none"`, metres, the release recorded.
- **SM-F3 — UVs are written at real size** from each article's `meters_per_tile`. So an article's tile size decides how often it repeats across a 5 m floor tile or a 7 m roof panel. That is why the sizes below matter.
- **SM-F4 — A sketch of it already validates against this repo's tools,** bound to the released articles `ABS_Matte`, `Concrete_Smooth_Worn_Dusty`, `Limestone_Veined`, `Copper_Verdigris_Aged` and `Neon_Signage`: `usdchecker` succeeds, `usdcat --flatten` composes with no errors, a tinted instance passes `check_lcd_carrier.py`, and it renders in Storm with the materials on. Copper and neon are stand-ins for the first two asks below.
- **SM-F5 — The emissive colour is the article's, not the Creator's** ([LCDSchema](../../specs/Contract/LCDSchema.md): `emission_color` is not a Creator port). So a white light strip cannot be reached by tinting `Neon_Signage`; it has to be its own article.

- **SM-F6 — Each is one substance, not an assembly.** Floor tiles, panels and seams are geometry on the platform's side; the asks are for the matter only (the generator skill's D1 rule).
- **SM-F7 — The wear layers are wanted as the draft list has them,** at zero. The Set should read clean; the app dials wear up.

### The tooling ask

- **SM-F8 — The profile gate cannot pass a one-mesh asset.** `tools/conformance/assert_profile.py` requires at least two meshes in two of its checks (`:62` `n_mesh >= 2`, `:67` `n_st >= 2`). It was shaped around the two-mesh golden table. A module file with one mesh, one binding and one `primvars:st` — which is what the profile asks of a single-region asset — fails with *"1 meshes vs 1 bindings"*. On the platform's sketch all 36 module files pass the other seven checks and fail these two.
  - **A7 — let the gate pass an asset that holds a single mesh,** keeping the "every mesh has exactly one binding and one `st`" assertion.

### What the platform needs back

- The article names as generated, and each one's `meters_per_tile`.
- A way for a draft to reach a consumer before it is in a release. The sketch records `matterlib-0.1.0`, and none of the six is in it.

### Open questions for the library

- **SM-Q1 — Brushed or raw first?** A brushed finish leans on `specular_roughness_anisotropy`, which `PlatformDependencies.md` P16 records as not yet carried on Opaque by the platform. Raw aluminium (values only) would render correctly everywhere today; brushed is the look that is wanted.
- **SM-Q2 — The floor's tile size.** The draft row is `s1` (1 m). On a 5 m floor tile that is 25 repeats per tile. Is a larger real tile available from a CC0 scan, or is the answer a larger scale tag?
- **SM-Q3 — How bright is a light strip?** What `emission_luminance` makes an LED read as a light source next to a sunlit surface, in the same convention `Neon_Signage` uses?
- **SM-Q4 — Drafts before a release.** Is the dev install (`PlatformDependencies.md` P19) the intended way for a consumer to see these four, and what does a Set record as its release while it uses them?
