# Phase15 — Scale Grids

**Status:** DISCOVERY, Pass 1 (2026-10-14). Numbered Phase15 by the lead: *"/discovery P15"*. Seeded 2026-10-14 as a pointer-shell written by `/research` on the lead's word: *"a Material for every UV scale that "aligns" with eachtoehr … we can use for testing when apllying UVs to ensure they are to scale … can be it's own phase"*. `/discovery` fills it. **Starting discovery is a deliberate gate, the lead's call, never a slide.**

## Outcome

**Anyone applying UVs can swap every object in a scene to the scale grid of its own material and see at once whether a metre is a metre: a wrong scale is obvious.**

## Why this is a phase

Today a material's size is checked in one place: the rig's ruler check, a number for one article in one test scene. Errors happen in UVs and bindings on real meshes, and nothing shows them by eye: a mesh's UVs in the wrong unit, a binding that forgot its UV scale, an object imported at the wrong size, or an article with no recorded size. The skin detail once drawn about 170 times too large on a character's own atlas was one of these ([LCDSchema](../../../specs/Contract/LCDSchema.md) §Creator-adjustable subset). The work is a new generator, six materials, a swap tool and a Blender toggle. That is more than one sitting.

## Scope — as ruled at research (2026-10-14)

The authority until this phase writes its specs: [`261014_R_ScaleProbes.md`](../../Research/261014_R_ScaleProbes.md) §Resolved (RD-SP-1) and its design sketch.

- **One scale grid for every scale tag**, `s0001` to `s100`: six materials, all aligned. A 100 m grid's finest line is the metre.
- **The colours and lines** (RD-SP-4):
  - A line's colour is its length, the same in every grid (for example, blue = 1 m).
  - A grid's background is a dark shade of its own scale's colour.
  - Each grid draws four line thicknesses: its tile edge, then each tenth below it, three times.
- **A grid for `sUKN`:** a hatch that reads *"size unknown"*, so an article with no recorded size stands out.
- **Generated by a script**, as the UV grid is (`tools/converters/gen_uvgrid.py`): deterministic, CC0 by construction, with the script as provenance.
- **The swap ("scale view"):** each bound article is replaced by the grid nearest its size. The binding's UV scale is corrected for the article's real `meters_per_tile`, because 15 of 94 recipes tile at a size other than their tag. The UV offset and rotation are kept. It runs in the rig and in the Blender add-on.
- **The 1 m reference cube** in the conformance golden (`13528ea`) wears the 1 m grid. That needs `tools/conformance/build_portable.py` to carry more than one article.
- **The word:** the Glossary's *Scale grid* headword. *Probe* is taken.
- **They ship in releases** (RD-SP-2): library content, so every consumer can offer a scale view when troubleshooting.
- **One name, except the scale tag** (RD-SP-3): for example `Diagnostic_ScaleGrid_Clean_Base_<sNN>_v01`. The exact tokens are checked against [Identity](../../../specs/Ontology/Identity.md).

## Open questions (settle during this phase)

- Where they sit: `utility/virtual/`, beside the UV grid, is the lean.
- The colour palette: one colour for each length any grid draws, from the `s0001` grid's finest line to 100 m, readable to a colour-blind eye.
- Whether the fourth, finest level is legible (about 4 px a cell at 4096 px), or fades with distance.
- Where the first proof runs: a character, where the 170× error lived, and the conformance table.
