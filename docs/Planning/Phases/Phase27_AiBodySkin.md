### What this phase builds, in one paragraph

**The body's skin comes from the model's picture of a person; every seam is decided on the mesh.** hm08's body is one island holding the trunk, arms and legs (58 % of the skin), laid out as a person seen from the front with the back split down the spine to the island's outer edges; the hands and feet are their own islands (F-P27-1, F-P27-3). Phase25's method carries over where a camera can see: a picture matched to hm08's render by landmarks and its silhouette, projected through that one camera, and one coordinate field carrying the rest, nothing blended (RD-P25-15, F-P25-16). **What does not carry over is the back:** no front camera sees it. Pass 2 measured the routes (§The routes): the model keeps hm08's pose and build best when it repaints hm08's own render shown **four times in one picture** (front, profile, back, profile), and each body texel then takes its colour from the view that faces it most squarely (F-P27-11). The lead judged that picture *"really good"* and its projection onto hm08 misaligned (RD-P27-5): the model's outline differs from hm08's in each view, so **each view is fitted to hm08's render (landmarks and the outline) before it is projected** (F-P27-12). That fit is 27.1's work and its click. **The four-view picture is too coarse for the skin itself** (1.67 mm a pixel; at click 2 it read as *"no effect at all"*, 27.2.2), and a close camera repainting a region redraws the body's marks (F-P27-18). So **the skin is split by size** (RD-P27-6): everything coarser than ~3 mm — the person's colour, the marks, the levels — is the four-view picture's, and the skin finer than that comes from **one close camera per region** (hm08 rendered at ~0.4 mm a pixel wearing D, repainted by Krea at 0.65), as a ratio on D. Regions cannot step against each other: their levels are all D's. The judge is the look view with an **unclothed** cast and body cameras, which do not exist yet (F-P27-5). Everything at 2K first (RD-P25-20), ~~which a 2 MP full-body picture already matches on the body (F-P27-4)~~ *(false for the four-view picture, 1.67 mm a pixel: 27.2.2, F-P27-14)*. The genitals and groin stay the maker's plain skin (RD-SKM-4).
